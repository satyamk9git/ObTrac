SOLSTICE ROBOTICS, INC.
Q3 2026 Earnings Summary — Fiscal Quarter Ended September 30, 2026
(Unaudited internal summary, prepared for the investor relations portal)

SECTION 1 — COMPANY OVERVIEW

Solstice Robotics, Inc. designs, manufactures, and services autonomous warehouse
picking robots under the AetherArm product line. The company's flagship product,
the AetherArm-7, is a six-axis robotic picking arm paired with a proprietary
vision-guided grasping system, sold primarily to mid-size third-party logistics
(3PL) warehouses and regional distribution centers across North America. Solstice
was founded in Fremont, California, and operates a single manufacturing facility
in Reno, Nevada, along with a regional service and spare-parts depot in Columbus,
Ohio. The company's business model combines an upfront hardware sale with an
ongoing software-and-maintenance subscription attached to each deployed unit.
Solstice competes in the broader warehouse-automation hardware category, where
demand has been shaped by continued e-commerce fulfillment growth and persistent
warehouse labor shortages. Management describes the AetherArm-7 as the centerpiece
of the company's near-term strategy, with future product lines still in early
internal design review and not yet ready for disclosure. This summary reports
results for the third fiscal quarter of 2026, covering the three-month
period from July 1, 2026 through September 30, 2026.

SECTION 2 — Q3 2026 FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Solstice Robotics reported revenue of
$58.4 million, gross margin of 61%, and net income of $6.1 million. Operating
cash flow for the quarter was $9.8 million. Total AetherArm-7 units shipped
during Q3 2026 were 1,240, at an average selling price of approximately $42,000
per unit, including the attached first-year software subscription. The company
ended the quarter with 187 active warehouse deployments, representing customers
with at least one AetherArm-7 unit installed and operating. Customer churn
during Q3 2026 — defined as customers that discontinued their software
subscription and did not renew — was 3.2%. Research and development spend
during the quarter was $11.2 million, or approximately 19% of quarterly revenue.
Selling, general and administrative expenses for the quarter were $8.7 million.
The company's cash and short-term investments balance stood at $74.5 million as
of September 30, 2026.

SECTION 3 — PRODUCT AND OPERATIONS UPDATE (Q3 2026)

During the third quarter of 2026, Solstice shipped firmware version 4.3 to the
AetherArm-7 fleet, which the engineering team says improved average pick-cycle
time and reduced misgrasp rate on soft-packaged items. The company also began
shipping an optional heavy-duty gripper attachment designed for palletized
goods above 15 kilograms, available as a paid add-on starting in August 2026.
Three new warehouse operators signed multi-unit deployment agreements during
the quarter: LogiCore Distribution, Fenwick Freight Systems, and Turnkey Pallet
Co., all announced via press release in August 2026. Installation of units for
these three new accounts is expected to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026.
The Reno manufacturing facility operated at approximately 78% of rated capacity
during Q3 2026, and the company noted no material supply chain disruptions
during the quarter. The Columbus service depot completed 340 field service
visits in Q3 2026, with a median technician response time of 1.8 business days.
The company also piloted a remote-diagnostics dashboard with a small subset of
customers during the quarter, with a broader rollout under internal evaluation.

Based on current bookings and the three new deployment agreements signed in
